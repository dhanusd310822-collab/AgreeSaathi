PRADHAN MANTRI FASAL BIMA YOJANA (PMFBY) - OFFICIAL GUIDELINES SUMMARY

1. OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVES:
PMFBY provides comprehensive crop insurance coverage against non-preventable natural risks from pre-sowing to post-harvest stages.

2. PREMIUM RATES FOR FARMERS:
- Kharif Crops (Food crops & Oilseeds): Maximum 2.0% of Sum Insured.
- Rabi Crops (Food crops & Oilseeds): Maximum 1.5% of Sum Insured.
- Commercial & Horticultural Crops: Maximum 5.0% of Sum Insured.

3. CRITICAL DEADLINE FOR CROP LOSS INTIMATION:
- In case of localized calamities (e.g., inundation, hailstorm, landslide) or post-harvest losses, loss intimation MUST be reported by the farmer within 72 HOURS of the event.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR CLAIM PROCESSING:
1. Aadhaar Card
2. Land Record Document (RTC / Pahani)
3. Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook
4. Sowing Certificate issued by Village Revenue Officer
